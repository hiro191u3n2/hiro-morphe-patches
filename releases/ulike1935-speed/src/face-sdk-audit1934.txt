Implemented conservative subset:
FaceRegions1934 detects eye midpoint/distance/pose from the exact saved
Bitmap using Android's bundled FaceDetector on an upright RGB_565 thumbnail
(max edge 640 pixels, even width). FaceRegions1934Pixels makes feathered,
conservative cheek/forehead eligibility plus broad eye/brow/mouth/outline
protection. A color/detail veto can only reduce these regions; it cannot
label skin-colored background outside a detected face as face skin.
No facial prior for low confidence, profiles, overlap, unsupported native
library, allocation/drawing failure, or no detected face. Same immutable
Mask tracks exact rotation/crop/resample pixel-center mapping.

Not implemented / must not claim:
- Precise semantic skin/eye/lip/hair parsing.
- Hair segmentation or precise lip/eyebrow contours from a saved-image model.
- SDK landmarks bound to exact still-image timestamp/crop/beauty transform.
- Android/Galaxy real-world FaceDetector accuracy or timing measured.

Host verification:
